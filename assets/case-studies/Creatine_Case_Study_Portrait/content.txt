A NUTRIFACTOR CREATINE CASE STUDY Get the Best to Be the Best. Role   Concept · Script · Set direction · Talent direction · Edit direction Brand   Nutrifactor     Product   Creatine Monohydrate Format   Cinematic gym film, Instagram + paid ads A cinematic gym film where a bodybuilder lifts hard, then talks straight to you. TAKE 2 · THE BRIEF Creatine is one of the most talked-about supplements in fitness, and the gym crowd can spot a generic ad instantly. My job was to introduce Nutrifactor’s Creatine Monohydrate to both serious athletes and regular gym-goers, and make it feel as credible and strong as the people who use it. TAKE 3 · THE IDEA Shoot it like a film. Say it like a coach. I shot the whole video in a real gym with a bodybuilder, cutting between cinematic workout shots and moments of him sitting and talking straight to the camera. The lifting brings the energy, the talking brings the message, and together it plays as one seamless video. I skipped a product-only ad. A real athlete holding the bottle makes the product credible. TAKE 4 · THE SCRIPT, CONDENSED BEAT WHAT HAPPENS WHY IT’S THERE The intro “Introducing Creatine Monohydrate.” He holds the bottle out to camera as the camera pushes in. A bold product-first opening that stops the scroll The brand tag “By Pakistan No.1 Vitamin Brand Nutrifactor.” B-roll of the bottle from different angles. Adds brand trust while the visuals keep moving The two audiences “Whether you are a high performance athlete or just a regular guy…” On screen: High Performance, Regular Person. Everyone in the gym sees themselves in the first 10 seconds The benefits Helps increase muscle mass and recovery, stamina and endurance, and boosts cognitive function. Benefits stacked in order, ending on the surprise one The close “If you are serious about your fitness journey, get the best to be the best.” A line that works as both the CTA and a tagline A note on claims:  benefits are phrased as “helps”. [Confirm the “Pakistan No.1 Vitamin Brand” line is backed by a source.] TAKE 5 · BEHIND THE GYM The set.  A real gym lit like a film set, with teal and red tones, neon light strips and moody shadows.  The camera.  A push-in as he holds the bottle out, then b-roll of the bottle and close-ups from different angles.  The performance.  A bodybuilder who trained on camera, then talked straight to it, cutting between the two without losing energy.  The edit.  Bold red and white on-screen words (High Performance, Regular Person, Recovery) and a closing “Get the Best to be the best” card carry the message. TAKE 6 · OUT IN THE WORLD WHERE IT RAN Posted on Nutrifactor’s Instagram, then handed to the media buying team to run as paid ads. BUILT FOR THE FEED A bold opening with the bottle held to camera, big on-screen words that work with the sound off, and a closing line that doubles as a tagline.
