A NUTRIFACTOR × CELLCENSE CASE STUDY Inside Out, Side by Side. Role   Concept · Script · Set direction · Talent direction · Edit direction Brand   Nutrifactor × Cellcense     Range   Supplements + skincare Format   Brand video, Instagram + paid ads TAKE 2 · THE BRIEF Cellcense was a brand-new skincare line, and people already trusted Nutrifactor’s beauty supplements. My job was to introduce Cellcense as part of the same family and make supplements and skincare feel like one routine, not two separate purchases. It was a brand video, so it had to say “these belong together” and make it stick. TAKE 3 · THE IDEA Inside on one side. Outside on the other. Instead of listing products, I built the video around pairing. It opens with combinations everyone already agrees on, like tea with rain, so supplement plus serum feels just as natural. The screen and the table are split in two colours, so the viewer sees the idea before the host says it. I chose not to do a product-by-product catalogue. A set of matches is easier to follow and easier to remember. TAKE 4 · THE SCRIPT, CONDENSED BEAT WHAT HAPPENS WHY IT’S THERE The hook “Some things just belong together, right?” Tea with rain, fries with ketchup. Familiar pairings explain the concept before any product appears The split The host points left to Nutrifactor supplements and right to the new Cellcense line. Introduces both sides and sets the visual logic for everything after The pairings One supplement for the inside, one Cellcense product for the outside, one-line benefit each. E.g. Butex + Hyaluronic &amp; Collagen Serum. The same pattern every time: learn it once, follow the rest The sign-off “Your inside-out combos that just work. With Nutrifactor and Cellcense.” Ties everything back to both brands in one line A note on tone:  written in a friendly, conversational voice (“match made in glow heaven”), like a friend recommending products rather than a brochure. TAKE 5 · BEHIND THE SPLIT The set.  Coloured paper covered the table, one half white and one half blue, matching the split background in the edit. It also matched the brand themes and showed the split. The camera.    It was shot in  static way with host being divided in two along two halves of the video to sell the differences but also the unique ways in which the two brands work together  [ photo ] The performance.  The host had to point left and right and handle products while staying warm through a long script The edit.  The background splits into white and blue with a line down the middle, so the set and graphics match and the inside-vs-outside idea holds on screen.  TAKE 6 · BUILT TO BEND The pairing structure is modular. Each combo is a small unit that can be swapped, added or removed without rewriting the video, and could be cut into shorter clips, one per pairing, for Stories, Reels or product ads.  TAKE 7 · OUT IN THE WORLD The video went live on Nutrifactor’s Instagram and was handed to the media buying team for paid campaigns. I built it as a brand piece: one clear idea, consistent visuals, and both brand names at the start and end.
