--- SLIDE 1 ---
A NUTRIFACTOR FERO CASE STUDY Stand Up. Spin. Sit Down. Role   Concept · Script · Set direction · Talent direction · Edit direction Brand   Nutrifactor     Product   Fero (iron supplement) Format   Instagram video + paid ads One person. One sofa. One very bad decision to stand up too fast. TAKE 2 · THE BRIEF Ramadan is when people become very aware of their energy, and that one head rush after standing up is a feeling almost everyone knows. My job was to connect that moment to low iron and Nutrifactor Fero TAKE 3 · THE IDEA Don't explain dizziness. Put the viewer inside it. Most supplement videos talk about symptoms. I wanted the viewer to feel one. Using gimbal movement, camera shake, and a blur effect, the audience experiences the dizziness through Sarah's eyes, and the product message lands after they've already felt the problem. I skipped the talking-head explainer because it looks like every other health video on the feed. A woman standing up and the whole screen tilting doesn't. TAKE 4 · THE SCRIPT, ANNOTATED BEAT WHAT HAPPENS WHY IT'S THERE The setup "This is Sarah, she is fasting." A relatable character and a timely context in one line The tease "She doesn't know it yet, but she's about to feel so dizzy due to low iron levels." A curiosity hook that makes people stay for the payoff The moment Sarah stands. The camera shakes and blurs. The symptom is shown, not told The twist "Yes. sit down." The voiceover reacts like a friend watching it happen, which adds humour and personality The turn "Don't be like her. Support your iron levels this Ramadan with Nutrifactor Fero." A clear CTA with a seasonal tie-in A note on claims:  the video suggests low iron can cause dizziness. It doesn't diagnose or promise a cure, which keeps it engaging without overstepping for a health product. TAKE 5 · BEHIND THE WOBBLE The set.  A simple sofa setup that looks like a real living room, because the dizziness needed to feel like something that could happen to anyone at home.  The camera.  I planned the gimbal movement with the videographer so the shake would start exactly as Sarah stands, not before The performance.  The challenge was getting a believable "head rush" without overacting.  The edit.  I guided the editor on the vertigo and blur effect, the on-screen text, and the sound cues.  TAKE 6 · BUILT TO BEND I wrote the "fasting" line so it could be swapped out. Change that one line and the same video works outside Ramadan, with Sarah reading late, skipping meals, or just standing up too quickly. One shoot, more than one season. TAKE 7 · OUT IN THE WORLD The video went live on Nutrifactor's Instagram and was handed to the media buying team for paid campaigns. I built it for that: a hook that works in the first seconds, a visual that works with the sound off, and one clear call  to action.
