A NUTRIFACTOR Vit. B12 CASE STUDY Chai. Energy. Crash. Role   Concept · Script · Set direction · Talent direction · Edit direction Brand   Nutrifactor     Product   BZOLVE (Vit. B12)  Format   Instagram video + paid ads TAKE 2 · THE BRIEF Chai/ Coffe  is a daily habit for a lot of people, and the mid-day energy dip is a feeling almost everyone knows. My job was to show that loop honestly, then introduce Nutrifactor BZOLVE as something to support energy levels. TAKE 3 · THE IDEA Show the loop before you sell the fix. Chai, Energy, Crash. Three words, three icons, and the whole cycle is understood in four seconds. Then the cuts repeat, faster each time, so the loop tightens on screen. A rewind animation resets the video and takes us back to Sarah, who thinks chai solves everything. Instead of opening with the product, I opened with a habit the viewer recognises, and let the product arrive as the answer to it. TAKE 4 · THE SCRIPT, ANNOTATED TIME · BEAT WHAT HAPPENS WHY IT’S THERE 0:00 The hook Person gets up abruptly and asks for one more chai. Starts mid-action with a question that pulls you in 0:03–0:07 The loop “Chai” + tea cup icon. “Energy” + icon with current animation. “Crash” with hazard sign. The whole cycle in four seconds, and does the emotional work After 0:07 The speed-up Quick cuts of Chai, Energy, Crash repeat, faster each time, with new clips. The tightening loop holds attention and shows the habit repeating 0:12 The rewind Rewind animation with a sound effect. A pattern interrupt: “let’s see how we got here” 0:14–0:22 The character “This is Sarah. Chai would solve everything. Even low energy.” A relatable character, and her belief in her own words 0:22–0:26 The reveal “Let’s see… Tadaaaa… NOPE!” Build-up and comic deflation, sold by the sound effects 0:27 The verdict “Do not be like her.” The voice reacts like a friend, and sets up the turn 0:31 The turn “Sometimes you need something better. Support your energy levels with Nutrifactor BZOLVE.” Product slide. Clear CTA and a product slide to land the brand A note on claims:  the copy says “support your energy levels”. It doesn’t promise a fix or a cure, which keeps it engaging without overstepping for a health product. TAKE 5 · BEHIND THE CUT [ photo ] The set.    We kept the set minimal with white background and white table. The whole point was to focus on the action.  	 The camera:  Guided the videographer to use both the gimbal shot tilting around in a helicopter shot as seen in the movies with a mix of static shot to clearly show the action. The performance.  Took a lot of tries guided the host was guided to give natural expression and put in a little exaggeration to add a little comic twist to the fact we do act like that when we are working on a short deadline.  [ photo ] The edit.  I guided the editor on the icon for each word, the current animation, the crash colour, the speed-up of the loop, and the rewind animation with its sound effect.  TAKE 6 · The pacing psychology Starting from mid-way and accelerating the clips mimics the feeling of caffeine anxiety, and the sudden rewind acts as a "breath" that resets the viewer's attention just as they might have scrolled away. TAKE 7 · OUT IN THE WORLD The video went live on Nutrifactor’s Instagram and was handed to the media buying team for paid campaigns. I built it for that: an abrupt, question-led opening, on-screen words and icons that carry the message, and a product slide with one clear call to action.  CRASHzzzzz
