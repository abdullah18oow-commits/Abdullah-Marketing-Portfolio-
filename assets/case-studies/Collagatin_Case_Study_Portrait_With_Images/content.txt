A NUTRIFACTOR COLLAGATIN CASE STUDY Water vs. Water Pro Max. Role   Concept · Script · Set direction · Talent direction · Edit direction Brand   Nutrifactor     Product   Collagatin (marine collagen) Format   Instagram video + paid ads Two glasses. Two personalities. One clear winner. TAKE 2 · THE BRIEF Collagatin is a marine collagen supplement, and collagen is a crowded, jargon-heavy category. Meanwhile, people already believe in one simple habit for glowing skin: drinking water. My job was to make Collagatin feel like the obvious upgrade to something people already do, in a feed where people see similar information on loop. TAKE 3 · THE IDEA Give the habit a personality. Let the upgrade win. Instead of explaining collagen, I put water and Collagatin in a face-off. Water gets a deep, dramatic voice and a plain white side. Collagatin gets a cheery voice and a bright pink one. The visual contrast tells the story before a single benefit is said. I skipped the straight benefits-list explainer. A rivalry is easier to watch and easier to remember. TAKE 4 · THE SCRIPT, CONDENSED BEAT WHAT HAPPENS WHY IT’S THERE The setup “This… is a glass of water.” (deep, dramatic) “And this… is Water Pro Max, with Nutrifactor’s Collagatin.” (cheery) Two voices, two moods: the contrast is set in seconds The look Water on the left against plain white. Collagatin on the right against bright pink. Each side has its own title. Viewers know who is who at a glance, and pink reads as the upgrade The comebacks Water boasts (“the mountain spring in a cup”). Collagatin answers with a benefit: 6000mg marine collagen, fine lines, hair, joints, gut. Benefits arrive as comebacks, not a list The banter “I am the OG.” / “And I’m the Upgrade.” “I’m the legend.” / “And I’m the Science.” A rhythmic call and response that escalates and is easy to quote The wink Collagatin winks and takes a sip. A small character moment that lands the win with charm The turn “Water is great, but sometimes you need something better.” End card: “Water + Collagatin = Beauty at its finest.” Respects water, so the CTA is “add”, not “replace” A note on claims:  benefits are phrased as “helps” and “supports”, and the video never puts water down. Collagatin is positioned as an add-on to water, not a replacement. TAKE 5 · BEHIND THE FACE-OFF The set.  A clean white side for Water and a bright pink side for Collagatin, split down the middle.  The camera.  Vertical framing, a locked split-screen for the face-off and a single-subject frame for the product finish The performance.  Two moods, two looks: a deadpan, offended Water in grey and black, and a cheery Collagatin in stripes and white, I directed the host to act like she is in a debate and loosing and winning at the same time. The edit.  On-screen text lands each claim (6000mg, Joints feel amazing), sparkles punctuate Collagatin, and a rising meter with an angry face shows Water losing its cool.  TAKE 6 · OUT IN THE WORLD WHERE IT RAN Posted on Nutrifactor’s Instagram, then handed to the media buying team to run as paid ads. BUILT FOR THE FEED A face-off that reads in one glance. White against pink works, and the closing line gives one clear next step. NEXT TIME I would like a plain shots as well listing the benefits so that it can be run as standalone video for the ads.
